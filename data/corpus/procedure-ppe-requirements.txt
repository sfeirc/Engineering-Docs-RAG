SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE DOCUMENT -- Generic Safety Procedure
Title: Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Requirements

Purpose
This generic procedure lists baseline and task-specific personal protective equipment for personnel working in or visiting a process area. Synthetic and illustrative only; it does not represent a specific site's actual PPE matrix.

Baseline PPE (all process areas)
- Hard hat, safety glasses with side shields, high-visibility clothing, safety footwear with steel or composite toe protection, and hearing protection where posted.

Task-specific additions
- Confined space entry: full-body harness with retrieval line (see Confined Space Entry procedure), and supplied-air or self-contained breathing apparatus if the atmosphere cannot be made and kept safe by ventilation alone.
- Hot work: flame-resistant coveralls, welding gloves and face shield/welding helmet appropriate to the process (see Hot Work Permit procedure).
- Work near H2S-containing streams: personnel must carry a personal H2S monitor and have immediate access to escape breathing apparatus (see H2S Emergency Response procedure).
- Chemical handling (e.g. cooling water treatment chemicals for Heat Exchanger HX-500, foam concentrate for Storage Tank TK-600): chemical-resistant gloves and face shield/goggles per the specific chemical's safety data sheet.

General rule
Task-specific PPE is always in addition to, not a replacement for, the baseline PPE above, unless a documented risk assessment specifically states otherwise for a defined task.

This is a synthetic example procedure created for a retrieval-augmented-generation demo corpus. It is illustrative only and must not be used as an actual operating instruction.
