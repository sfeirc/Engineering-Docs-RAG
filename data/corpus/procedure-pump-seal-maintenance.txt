SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE DOCUMENT -- Generic Maintenance Procedure
Title: Centrifugal Pump Mechanical Seal Maintenance Procedure

Purpose
This generic procedure describes routine inspection and replacement of a cartridge-style mechanical seal on a centrifugal pump such as Pump CP-100. Synthetic and illustrative only.

Routine inspection
1. Visually inspect the seal area for visible leakage, weeping, or crystallized product buildup at every operator round.
2. Monitor the seal flush system (e.g. Plan 11 flush as fitted to Pump CP-100) for correct flow and pressure differential per the pump's datasheet.
3. Log any detected leakage, however minor, for trending; a seal that has started leaking rarely stops on its own and the trend matters more than any single reading.

Seal replacement (requires equipment shutdown and isolation)
1. Isolate and lock out the pump per the Lockout-Tagout procedure; drain the casing and depressurize the seal chamber.
2. Remove the coupling guard and coupling, then withdraw the cartridge seal assembly per the manufacturer's instructions, taking care not to damage the shaft sleeve.
3. Inspect the shaft sleeve and stuffing box bore for wear, scoring, or corrosion before installing the replacement seal.
4. Install the new cartridge seal, verify the setting dimension against the manufacturer's specification, and reassemble the coupling with a proper alignment check.
5. Restore the flush system, remove isolation, and start the pump per normal start-up procedure, then monitor the new seal closely for the first 24 hours of operation.

This is a synthetic example procedure created for a retrieval-augmented-generation demo corpus. It is illustrative only and must not be used as an actual operating instruction.
