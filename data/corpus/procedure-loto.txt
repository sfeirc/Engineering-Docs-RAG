SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE DOCUMENT -- Generic Safety Procedure
Title: Lockout-Tagout (LOTO) Procedure for Rotating and Pressurized Equipment

Purpose
This generic procedure describes the minimum steps required to isolate rotating and pressurized equipment (such as pumps and compressors) from all hazardous energy sources before maintenance work begins. It is a synthetic, illustrative example and does not represent any specific company's actual controlled procedure.

Scope
Applies to any equipment where unexpected start-up, release of stored energy, or movement of parts could injure personnel, including but not limited to Pump CP-100, Compressor RC-200 and Compressor CC-300 described elsewhere in this corpus.

Procedure steps
1. Notify affected operations personnel and review the equipment's isolation points using the equipment isolation drawing.
2. Shut down the equipment through normal operating controls.
3. Isolate all energy sources: electrical supply at the breaker/disconnect, process isolation valves (block and bleed where required), and any pneumatic or hydraulic supply.
4. Apply personal locks and tags to each isolation point. Each individual working on the equipment applies their own personal lock; no work proceeds under someone else's lock alone.
5. Release stored energy: bleed trapped process pressure to a safe location, block rotating parts, and allow hot surfaces/fluids to cool to a safe handling temperature.
6. Verify isolation ("try before you rely"): attempt to start the equipment through normal controls and confirm it does not operate; verify zero energy state with appropriate instruments (e.g. voltage tester, pressure gauge).
7. Perform the work.
8. On completion, remove tools, restore guards, and account for all personnel before removing locks in the reverse order they were applied.

Every lock removed by anyone other than the person who applied it requires a documented, authorized lock-removal procedure -- this is not a routine step and must not become one.

This is a synthetic example procedure created for a retrieval-augmented-generation demo corpus. It is illustrative only and must not be used as an actual operating instruction.
