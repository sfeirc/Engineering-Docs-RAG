Title: Corrosion Monitoring Procedure

Purpose
This generic procedure describes routine corrosion monitoring feeding into the pressure vessel and piping inspection interval-setting process. Synthetic and illustrative only.

Monitoring methods
1. Corrosion coupons: metal samples of representative material exposed to the process stream and removed periodically for weight-loss measurement, giving a direct corrosion-rate estimate for that location.
2. Ultrasonic thickness (UT) monitoring: fixed measurement points on piping and vessels (e.g. on Storage Tank TK-600's shell courses and Heat Exchanger HX-500's shell) read on a set interval to trend wall-thickness loss over time.
3. Online corrosion probes (e.g. electrical resistance probes) at selected high-risk locations, providing a near-continuous corrosion-rate trend rather than periodic spot readings.

Using the results
A measured corrosion rate is compared against the design corrosion allowance for the component to estimate remaining life. A corrosion rate significantly higher than the design assumption triggers a review of the affected component's inspection interval and, where warranted, a metallurgical or process-chemistry investigation into the root cause -- it is not simply logged and left for the next scheduled inspection.

Reporting
Corrosion monitoring results feed directly into the interval-setting step of the Pressure Vessel Inspection Procedure; the two procedures are designed to be read together.
