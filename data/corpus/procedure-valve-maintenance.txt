Title: Control and Isolation Valve Maintenance Procedure

Purpose
This generic procedure covers routine maintenance of control valves (such as Control Valve CV-700) and manual isolation valves in process service. Synthetic and illustrative only.

Control valve maintenance
1. Review positioner diagnostics (where fitted) and partial stroke test results before any physical inspection.
2. Isolate and depressurize the valve per the Lockout-Tagout procedure before removing the actuator or trim.
3. Inspect the seat and plug/ball for erosion, wire-drawing, or seat leakage evidence; replace trim components showing wear beyond the manufacturer's allowable limits.
4. On reassembly, verify stroke time and fail-safe action (e.g. fail-open, as specified for Control Valve CV-700) before returning the valve to service.

Manual isolation valve maintenance
1. Exercise (fully cycle) manual block valves used in safety-critical isolation duties (e.g. relief valve block valves, if fitted and administratively controlled) on the interval specified by the site's valve criticality assessment.
2. Inspect valve packing for leakage at every operator round; re-pack or adjust the gland per the manufacturer's instructions if leakage is detected, following the Lockout-Tagout procedure if the work requires isolation.
3. Record all valve maintenance activity against the equipment's maintenance history for trend analysis and spares planning.
