SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE DOCUMENT -- Generic Safety Procedure
Title: Hot Work Permit Procedure

Purpose
This generic procedure covers the controls required before any hot work (welding, cutting, grinding, or any activity producing an open flame or spark) is performed in or near a process area. Synthetic and illustrative only.

Permit requirements
1. A hot work permit must be issued by an authorized area operator/authority before work begins, valid only for the specific location, task and shift stated on the permit.
2. A combustible gas test must be performed at the work location immediately before work starts, and must read 0% of the lower explosive limit; the test is repeated periodically for work lasting more than one shift or if conditions change.
3. All combustible materials within a defined radius of the work location must be removed or covered with fire-resistant blankets.
4. A fire watch with a charged extinguisher must be present for the entire duration of hot work and for a defined period after work stops (commonly referred to as a fire watch stand-down period).
5. If hot work is to be performed on equipment that was in hydrocarbon service (such as a section of piping near Pump CP-100 or Compressor RC-200), the equipment must first be isolated per the Lockout-Tagout procedure, drained, and gas-freed, with the gas test result recorded on the permit.

Restricted hot work
Some areas or conditions (for example, work directly on a live relief valve tail pipe, or work during specific high-risk operations elsewhere in the plant) may require additional area-specific authorization beyond the standard hot work permit -- this is determined case by case by the area authority, not assumed by the permit issuer.
